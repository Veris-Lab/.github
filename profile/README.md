## What you can build with Veris

**Global KYC and onboarding**
Capture and verify government-issued IDs (passport, national ID, driver's licence, residence permits), confirm liveness, and match faces in a single flow.

**Step-up authentication**
Replace weak methods like SMS OTP with biometric confirmation for high-risk actions - transfers, withdrawals, account changes.

**Account recovery and ownership verification**
Secure password resets and account recovery by verifying the real user, not just device or SIM access.

**Transaction authorization**
Require a quick face check before approving sensitive operations - large payments, wallet withdrawals, card issuance.

**Fraud prevention and risk control**
Detect spoofing attempts, prevent account takeovers, and reduce identity fraud with real-time verification signals.

**Compliance-ready audit trails**
Every verification result is cryptographically signed (ECDSA) - tamper-proof proof for audits and regulatory reviews.

**Cross-platform identity layer**
One identity signal across mobile apps, web apps, POS systems, and embedded devices.
